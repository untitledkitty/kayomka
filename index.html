About me

I am a young self‑taught artist, Kayomochka. I have many years of experience in both traditional and digital formats.

I create original characters and also work on their backstory. I derive great pleasure from developing my characters, putting my soul into them.

I’ll take on your character with even greater enthusiasm if you approached its creation with the same care, because I’m just as captivated by the inner workings as I am by the outward appearance.
